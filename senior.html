Macon County Department of Social Services

Senior Services - (828) 349-2058

Senior Services and the Community Resource Center is located at 108 Wayah Street in Franklin, NC. We offer a variety of programs to help older adults maintain their independence.

The Resource Center is a point of access for services available in the county. Office hours are Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Call 349-2101 for assistance.

Who is Eligible?

If you are eligible to receive services, contact the office to begin assessment and referral.

Programs and Services

  • SHIP: Medicare/Insurance counseling (Call 1-800-443-9354 or visit their website)
  • Adult Daycare: Crafts, light exercise, games, and licensed supervision
  • Senior Protection: Daily phone call check-ins for vulnerable seniors
  • Project Lifesaver: Radio transmitter program for high-risk individuals
  • Home Delivered Meals: Prepared meals delivered Mon–Fri (limited service area)
  • Congregate Meals: Meals served onsite with social and wellness activities
  • Volunteer Opportunities: Meal delivery, counseling, general aid

Note: Meal service availability may change based on weather. Local news stations will broadcast updates.
