Sheriff Robert L. Holland

Career Overview

Sheriff Holland's law enforcement career began in 1990 with volunteer service and progressed through various key roles:

  • Detention Officer
  • Animal Control Officer
  • Deputy Sheriff
  • Investigator
  • Detective Sgt. and Supervisor of the Sheriff's Office Juvenile Unit

He was instrumental in legislation like the Safe Surrender Law and received multiple awards, including the L.T. Watkins Juvenile Officer of the Year and the Donna B. Stone Memorial Award. He began his fifth term in 2002.
